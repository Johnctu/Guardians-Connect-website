YOUR RIGHTS & YOUR VOICE

Privacy notice

Your information deserves care. This notice explains how Guardians Connect handles personal information in enquiries, support delivery and this website.

Published 30 September 2026. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, contact info@guardiansconnect.com.au and ask for management.

Information we collect and why

When you contact us or receive support, we may collect your name, contact details, communication preferences, enquiry details, service agreements and records of support. Where needed for your support, this may include disability or health information, NDIS plan details, emergency contacts and information about your goals and safety needs.

We use relevant information to respond to enquiries, assess suitable support, plan and deliver services, manage payments, respond to feedback or complaints and meet applicable recordkeeping and reporting obligations. Please share only what is needed. You do not need to send medical records or your full NDIS plan in an initial enquiry.

Collection, choice and consent

We seek information directly from you where practicable. With your permission or other lawful authority, we may receive relevant information from your representative, support coordinator or other people involved in your support. We seek consent for sensitive information unless the law permits or requires collection without it. You can ask why information is needed, decline to provide it or discuss consent preferences; this may affect which services we can arrange.

Who information may be shared with

Information may be shared with workers involved in your support, authorised representatives, relevant service providers or administrative suppliers where necessary and permitted. We seek your permission where required and limit sharing to its purpose. Disclosure may also be required or permitted by law, including safeguarding, incident reporting and regulatory requirements. We do not sell your personal information.

Keeping information safe and retaining records

Our commitment is to take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and limit access to people who need it for their role. Records are retained for applicable legal and service requirements, then securely destroyed or de-identified when no longer required. Contact management to discuss the retention requirements or safeguards that apply to your records.

Website, enquiry downloads and voice

The website enquiry form currently creates a text file on your device; it does not submit the enquiry to Guardians Connect. To contact us, email us directly. A downloaded file remains on your device until you remove it. If you email it, your email provider and our email service process that message.

The automated service assistant uses prepared website information. Typed questions stay in page memory until you leave or refresh, and are not sent to Guardians Connect or saved in browser storage by the assistant. It is not a channel for private records, complaints or emergencies.

Optional voice features use browser speech services, which may process audio or text through the browser provider. Microphone use starts only when you choose Dictate question. Hosting providers may process technical access logs. This website does not add analytics or advertising tracking code.

If online enquiry delivery is enabled in future, FormSubmit will process the submitted details and forward them to info@guardiansconnect.com.au; the form notice will identify that change before submission. External links have their own privacy practices.

Service providers and overseas processing

Website hosting, email and optional browser speech services may involve processing outside Australia. Provider locations and arrangements can vary. Contact management for information about the providers and overseas disclosures relevant to your records; this notice does not identify specific overseas countries. We will explain applicable arrangements before collecting information for a new service where required.

Access, corrections and privacy concerns

You can ask to access your personal information, correct inaccurate details or raise a privacy concern using the contact details above. We may need to verify your identity or authority before providing records. We will explain the response, next steps and any lawful reason that limits access.

If you are dissatisfied, ask management to review the response. You may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) about matters within its jurisdiction. Its website explains eligibility and the process, including first raising your concern with the organisation. Concerns about privacy in NDIS supports can also be raised with the NDIS Commission through our feedback and complaints pathways.
