WHO WE ARE

Connection begins
with understanding.

Guardians Connect is a registered NDIS provider supporting people with disability in Metropolitan Perth, Western Australia. Our approved services span everyday support, community participation, home and living, equipment, employment, early childhood supports, coordination and plan management.

We believe good support begins by listening. Your priorities, communication preferences, culture and personal boundaries guide the conversation about your support.

Our focus is to help you build independence and stay connected to the people, places and activities that matter to you.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND OUR SUPPORT

Our Management
& Team

Our management team draws on qualifications and experience in healthcare, education and governance to guide Guardians Connect with care and accountability. We believe reliable support begins with understanding the individual, listening to their priorities and delivering on our commitments. Working alongside a dependable, compassionate team, we are committed to protecting participants’ wellbeing, respecting their choices and supporting their goals through thoughtful planning and open communication.

Website privacy

This website does not send your enquiry entries or service-assistant messages to Guardians Connect or save them in browser storage. The automated service guide uses prepared website information; typed messages stay in memory until you leave or refresh the page. Optional voice features use browser speech services, which may process audio or text. Microphone use starts only when you choose Dictate question. Downloading an enquiry creates a text file on your device. Hosting providers may process technical access logs. When online delivery is enabled, FormSubmit will process your enquiry and forward it to info@guardiansconnect.com.au. For privacy questions, email info@guardiansconnect.com.au. Read our business privacy notice for more information.
