Household Tasks

Support to keep everyday household routines manageable.

Enquire about this service

WHO THIS MAY SUPPORT

Start with your needs.

People who need assistance with household tasks because of their support needs. Tasks are agreed around your circumstances, preferences and goals.

We start by listening to your goals, preferences and communication needs. You can choose to involve a family member, advocate or trusted person.

WHAT ASSISTANCE CAN INVOLVE

Support shaped around you.

1

Routine cleaning

Discuss help with agreed household cleaning tasks and maintaining usable living spaces.

2

Laundry and home routines

Explore assistance with laundry and other everyday household activities.

3

Doing things together

Where suitable, work alongside a support worker to practise routines and build confidence.

The team confirms the agreed task list, service availability and any safety considerations. Specialist cleaning or maintenance should be discussed separately.

YOUR FIRST CONVERSATION

What to discuss
when you enquire.

  • The household tasks you need help with.
  • Your suburb, home environment and preferred support times.
  • Products, access arrangements or household preferences that matter to you.

Share a brief description to begin. Please do not include NDIS numbers, medical records or other sensitive documents in your initial message.

HELPFUL ANSWERS

Questions about
this service?

Can I choose the tasks?

Share your priorities. A clear task list is agreed before support begins.

Can the support include building my skills?

Discuss whether you would like to participate in tasks and practise household routines as part of your goals.

Website privacy

This website does not send your enquiry entries or service-assistant messages to Guardians Connect or save them in browser storage. The automated service guide uses prepared website information; typed messages stay in memory until you leave or refresh the page. Optional voice features use browser speech services, which may process audio or text. Microphone use starts only when you choose Dictate question. Downloading an enquiry creates a text file on your device. Hosting providers may process technical access logs. When online delivery is enabled, FormSubmit will process your enquiry and forward it to info@guardiansconnect.com.au. For privacy questions, email info@guardiansconnect.com.au.
