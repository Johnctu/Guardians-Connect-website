Personal Care & Daily Activities

Respectful assistance with your everyday personal routines.

Enquire about this service

WHO THIS MAY SUPPORT

Start with your needs.

People who need help with personal activities or daily routines and want support guided by their preferences, dignity and independence.

We start by listening to your goals, preferences and communication needs. You can choose to involve a family member, advocate or trusted person.

WHAT ASSISTANCE CAN INVOLVE

Support shaped around you.

1

Personal routines

Discuss assistance with activities such as dressing, grooming and other agreed personal care tasks.

2

Choice and comfort

Talk about your preferred routines, communication style and what helps you feel comfortable.

3

Support planning

Agree on the activities, timing, boundaries and assistance required before support begins.

The service scope and worker suitability are confirmed individually. This page does not describe high-intensity clinical care or guarantee that a particular task can be provided.

YOUR FIRST CONVERSATION

What to discuss
when you enquire.

  • The routines or activities you would like help with.
  • Your preferred days, times and any worker preferences.
  • Any relevant support instructions to discuss securely with the team.

Share a brief description to begin. Please do not include NDIS numbers, medical records or other sensitive documents in your initial message.

HELPFUL ANSWERS

Questions about
this service?

Can I discuss worker preferences?

Yes. Tell us what matters to you, including communication needs and personal preferences. Availability is discussed individually.

Can a trusted person help me enquire?

Yes. A family member, advocate or trusted person can assist with your permission.

Website privacy

This website does not send your enquiry entries or service-assistant messages to Guardians Connect or save them in browser storage. The automated service guide uses prepared website information; typed messages stay in memory until you leave or refresh the page. Optional voice features use browser speech services, which may process audio or text. Microphone use starts only when you choose Dictate question. Downloading an enquiry creates a text file on your device. Hosting providers may process technical access logs. When online delivery is enabled, FormSubmit will process your enquiry and forward it to info@guardiansconnect.com.au. For privacy questions, email info@guardiansconnect.com.au.
