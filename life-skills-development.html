Life Skills Development

Build practical skills at a pace that works for you.

Enquire about this service

WHO THIS MAY SUPPORT

Start with your needs.

People who want to build everyday skills, confidence and independence. Goals can be small, practical and shaped around the activities that matter most to you.

We start by listening to your goals, preferences and communication needs. You can choose to involve a family member, advocate or trusted person.

WHAT ASSISTANCE CAN INVOLVE

Support shaped around you.

1

Daily routines

Practise skills such as planning routines, preparing food or organising everyday activities.

2

Learning through participation

Break activities into manageable steps and discuss the support that helps you learn.

3

Your own goals

Choose the skills you want to work on and review progress through your support conversations.

The activities and level of assistance are tailored through an agreed support plan. Progress varies with each person’s goals and circumstances.

YOUR FIRST CONVERSATION

What to discuss
when you enquire.

  • The skills you want to develop and why they matter to you.
  • What you already do independently and where help is useful.
  • Your preferred learning style and any communication requirements.

Share a brief description to begin. Please do not include NDIS numbers, medical records or other sensitive documents in your initial message.

HELPFUL ANSWERS

Questions about
this service?

Can I work on one skill at a time?

Yes. Start with a priority that feels useful and manageable.

Do I need to know exactly what my goal is?

No. You can discuss the everyday activities you would like to feel more confident doing.
