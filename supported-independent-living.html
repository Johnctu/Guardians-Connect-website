Supported Independent Living

Everyday support to live with greater independence at home.

Enquire about this service

WHO THIS MAY SUPPORT

Start with your needs.

People who need assistance with daily routines at home, including those exploring shared living or a change in their support arrangements. The level and pattern of support are discussed individually.

We start by listening to your goals, preferences and communication needs. You can choose to involve a family member, advocate or trusted person.

WHAT ASSISTANCE CAN INVOLVE

Support shaped around you.

1

Everyday routines

Assistance with agreed personal activities, meals, household tasks and daily routines.

2

Building independence

Opportunities to practise skills and take an active role in decisions about your daily life.

3

Working together

Planning around your preferences, communication needs and the people you choose to involve.

An enquiry does not confirm housing availability or a particular staffing arrangement. We discuss the support scope, availability and agreement before services begin.

YOUR FIRST CONVERSATION

What to discuss
when you enquire.

  • Your current living arrangement and the changes you are considering.
  • The activities you need help with and when support is needed.
  • Your goals, preferences, available funding and any relevant assessments.

Share a brief description to begin. Please do not include NDIS numbers, medical records or other sensitive documents in your initial message.

HELPFUL ANSWERS

Questions about
this service?

Can my preferences be included?

Yes. Tell us about your routines, communication style, personal boundaries and preferences when planning support.

Can I enquire while exploring my options?

Yes. You can start a conversation before you have decided on a living arrangement.
