Support Coordination

Make sense of your support options and plan your next steps.

Enquire about this service

WHO THIS MAY SUPPORT

Start with your needs.

Participants who want help understanding their plan, connecting with providers or coordinating different supports. Family members and trusted people can be involved at the participant’s direction.

We start by listening to your goals, preferences and communication needs. You can choose to involve a family member, advocate or trusted person.

WHAT ASSISTANCE CAN INVOLVE

Support shaped around you.

1

Understanding options

Discuss your goals, available supports and questions about putting your plan into action.

2

Connecting with services

Explore providers and services that may suit your needs, location and preferences.

3

Keeping supports connected

Build your confidence in coordinating services and discussing changes when circumstances evolve.

Support coordination helps you explore and organise options. It does not guarantee funding decisions, provider availability or a particular outcome.

YOUR FIRST CONVERSATION

What to discuss
when you enquire.

  • The goals and support areas you want to work on.
  • Existing providers, gaps in support or upcoming changes.
  • How your plan is managed and your preferred way to communicate.

Share a brief description to begin. Please do not include NDIS numbers, medical records or other sensitive documents in your initial message.

HELPFUL ANSWERS

Questions about
this service?

Can I choose my own providers?

Your choices and preferences guide the conversation. You can discuss the providers you want to work with.

Is this the same as plan management?

No. Support coordination focuses on organising supports; plan management focuses on funding administration and provider invoices.

Website privacy

This website does not send your enquiry entries or service-assistant messages to Guardians Connect or save them in browser storage. The automated service guide uses prepared website information; typed messages stay in memory until you leave or refresh the page. Optional voice features use browser speech services, which may process audio or text. Microphone use starts only when you choose Dictate question. Downloading an enquiry creates a text file on your device. Hosting providers may process technical access logs. When online delivery is enabled, FormSubmit will process your enquiry and forward it to info@guardiansconnect.com.au. For privacy questions, email info@guardiansconnect.com.au.
