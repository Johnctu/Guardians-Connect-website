OUR APPROACH

Support built around
your version of life.

Guardians Connect is a registered NDIS provider focused on person-centred disability support in Metropolitan Perth, Western Australia. We start by listening to what matters to you.

You choose who is involved in planning your support, including family, trusted people or an advocate. Your culture, communication preferences and personal boundaries matter.

A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Questions?
Start here.

Do I need to know which service I want?

No. You can begin by sharing the activities you need help with or the goals you would like to work towards.

Can my family or advocate be involved?

Yes. You choose who you want involved in conversations about your support.

Can you support my NDIS funding arrangement?

Guardians Connect is a registered NDIS provider. Tell us whether your plan is agency-managed, plan-managed or self-managed so we can discuss your available funding and the services you need. Support and payment arrangements are confirmed before services begin.

Where is support available?

Guardians Connect serves Metropolitan Perth, Western Australia, Australia. Availability for your chosen service is confirmed when you enquire.

How can I give feedback or raise a concern?

You can raise questions or concerns with your support contact and ask for an advocate to assist you. For independent information about NDIS complaints, visit the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission.

Website privacy

This website does not send your enquiry entries or service-assistant messages to Guardians Connect or save them in browser storage. Optional voice features use browser speech services, which may process audio or text. Microphone use starts only when you choose Dictate question. Downloading an enquiry creates a text file on your device. Hosting providers may process technical access logs.
