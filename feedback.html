YOUR RIGHTS & YOUR VOICE

Feedback, complaints & advocacy

Your voice matters. Tell us what is working, suggest a change or raise a concern about your support. You can also seek independent help at any time.

Immediate danger or emergency

If someone is in immediate danger or needs emergency assistance, call 000. Our email, website assistant and enquiry download are not monitored emergency services.

Contact Guardians Connect

Email info@guardiansconnect.com.au. Ask for management and say whether you are providing feedback or making a complaint.

Contact hours: Monday - Sunday, by appointment. Service area: Metropolitan Perth, Western Australia, Australia. You can send an email at any time; these hours do not mean continuous monitoring.

Tell us what happened, when it happened, what you would like to change and how you prefer us to contact you. Share only necessary personal information. The website enquiry download and assistant do not send complaints to us.

How we respond

We welcome feedback and will treat concerns respectfully. Making a complaint will not disadvantage you or affect your right to safe, respectful support. We will listen, acknowledge your concern, discuss immediate safety needs and explain who will review it and the expected next steps. We will keep you informed, discuss the outcome and use feedback to improve support.

If you are unhappy with the response, ask management for a review. If your concern involves management, or you prefer independent help, contact the NDIS Commission directly. You do not need to complain to Guardians Connect first.

Support to speak up

You may involve a trusted person, family member, representative or independent advocate. Tell us if you need an interpreter, a different communication format or help explaining your concern. We will discuss suitable assistance with you and cooperate with your chosen advocate.

You can raise a concern without giving your name. Please be aware that email may identify you through your sender address; the NDIS Commission also offers anonymous reporting options. Without contact details we may be unable to ask follow-up questions or tell you the outcome. We handle complaints sensitively and limit sharing to people who need the information to respond or meet legal obligations; absolute confidentiality cannot always be guaranteed.

Independent complaints about NDIS supports

The NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission receives concerns about the quality and safety of NDIS providers and workers.

Report an issue to the NDIS Commission

Call 1800 035 544. In Western Australia, its published contact centre hours are Monday to Friday, 7:30am - 3:30pm, excluding public holidays. TTY: 133 677. Through the National Relay Service, ask for 1800 035 544. Interpreters can be arranged. Check the Commission website for current hours and reporting options.

NDIS plan funding and NDIA decision concerns follow a separate pathway: see NDIA contact information.

Find an independent advocate

An independent disability advocate can help you understand your rights, explain your concerns and speak or write on your behalf. Advocates are independent of Guardians Connect.

Find a disability advocate

Search for advocacy services in Perth or your location. You can also read the NDIS Commission’s guidance on help from an advocate.

Website privacy

This website does not send your enquiry entries or service-assistant messages to Guardians Connect or save them in browser storage. The automated service guide uses prepared website information; typed messages stay in memory until you leave or refresh the page. Optional voice features use browser speech services, which may process audio or text. Microphone use starts only when you choose Dictate question. Downloading an enquiry creates a text file on your device. Hosting providers may process technical access logs. When online delivery is enabled, FormSubmit will process your enquiry and forward it to info@guardiansconnect.com.au. For privacy questions, email info@guardiansconnect.com.au. Read our business privacy notice for more information.
